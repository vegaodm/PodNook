Getting started

Subscribe to a podcast

  1. Open Library and tap +.
  2. Paste the show’s RSS feed URL (for example https://feeds.npr.org/510289/podcast.xml).
  3. Tap Subscribe. PodNook fetches the feed once and stores it locally.

A website address will not work — it must be an RSS or Atom feed.

Import an OPML export

  1. Export your subscriptions as OPML from your other podcast app.
  2. Keep it anywhere in Files, or drop it into Files → On My iPhone → PodNook.
  3. Open PodNook → Settings → Import OPML and pick the file.
  4. PodNook imports three at a time, skips shows already in your library, and lists broken feeds at the end.

Rename a show

Open a show and tap the pencil in the header. “Use original name” restores the feed’s own title; the custom name is used in the library, the show page and on the lock screen.

Downloads in progress

While a download is running, the Downloads tab shows a small red dot. Open it to see every queued and running transfer under “In progress”, with progress and a cancel button; finished files sit below.

Downloaded episodes

Downloaded episodes appear in a Downloaded shelf at the top of the Library — one tap plays them offline. Manage opens the Downloads tab. The Downloads tab sorts by most recently downloaded.

Refresh

Pull down on the library, or tap the refresh button. PodNook also refreshes on launch and when you return after 15 minutes, using conditional requests so unchanged shows cost almost no data.

Playback

  • Background playback — keeps playing when locked or in another app.
  • Lock screen & Control Center — play/pause, back 15 s, forward 15 s, previous/next episode.
  • Headphones — unplugging pauses; a phone call resumes afterwards.
  • Speed — tap the speed chip or change it in Settings (0.8×–2×).
  • Resume — progress saved every 5 s; under 10 s of progress starts over.
  • Up Next — add episodes to the queue; the player lists what follows and jumps on tap.
  • Remaining time — the right side of the scrubber shows -mm:ss.

Downloads

  • Tap the arrow on an episode; progress shows in the row and in Downloads.
  • Downloads resume after losing the network or restarting the app.
  • Playback prefers the downloaded file, so offline listening needs no network.
  • Cancel in-flight downloads from the row; delete finished ones with the trash button.
  • Settings → Delete All Downloads clears audio; Clear Artwork Cache clears images.

Favorites & tabs

  • The pill bottom-left holds Library, Favorites and Downloads; the floating button bottom-right opens settings.
  • Swipe left or right on the main screen to cycle Library → Favorites → Downloads → Library; a liquid-glass highlight glides to the new tab and a soft tint sweeps the page.
  • Tap the heart in the player to keep an episode in Favorites.
  • Open the Favorites tab and tap an episode to play it with the rest of the list queued up.
  • While you scroll down the pill first shrinks into one icon on the left, mirroring settings on the right, then both icons hide and the list owns the bottom of the screen. Scrolling up brings the icons back; tap the icon or scroll to the top for the full pill.

Troubleshooting

SymptomWhat to try
“Update failed” in the libraryThe show’s server did not answer; pull to refresh later, the badge clears after a successful update.
Download says “Link expired”The media URL uses a temporary token; refresh the feed and retry.
Download stops part-wayTap retry; PodNook resumes from the bytes it has, or restarts if the host cannot resume.
Episode will not playCheck the connection. Some hosts block non-browser clients or only serve HTTP media.
OPML import finds nothingUse a .opml or .xml file containing xmlUrl attributes.
Black screen “In iOS 14+, debug mode Flutter apps…”That build is a Debug build launched from the home screen. Launch from Xcode/flutter run or install a Release build.
No lock-screen controlsStart an episode once inside the app; now-playing info is published on first play.
Covers missingSome publisher CDNs (for example NPR) refuse direct image requests with 403. A placeholder is shown, playback and downloads are unaffected, and the cover appears once it can be fetched — then it is cached for offline use.
Covers after going offlineCovers you have seen, and covers of downloaded episodes, are stored on disk and keep working without a connection.

FAQ

Does PodNook collect data? No — no account, tracking, ads or analytics. See the privacy policy.

Does it sync? Not in 1.1; everything is local.

CarPlay, sleep timer, chapters? Not in 1.1. Lock screen and Control Center controls are supported.

Can I export subscriptions? Import works; export is planned.

How much space do downloads use? Settings shows the total; an hour is typically 30–60 MB.

What happens if I delete the app? Library, progress and downloads are removed with it.

快速开始

订阅播客

  1. 打开 Library，点右上角 +。
  2. 粘贴节目的 RSS 地址（如 https://feeds.npr.org/510289/podcast.xml）。
  3. 点 Subscribe，PodNook 抓取一次并保存到本地。

普通网站地址不行，必须是 RSS/Atom feed。

导入 OPML

  1. 在原播客 App 中导出 OPML。
  2. 放在“文件”任意位置，或放进 文件 → 我的 iPhone → PodNook。
  3. PodNook → Settings → Import OPML，选择文件。
  4. 以 3 个并发导入，自动跳过已有订阅，失败的 feed 会列在结果里。

重命名节目

进入节目页点右上角铅笔修改名称；“Use original name” 可恢复 feed 原标题。 自定义名称会用于库、节目页与锁屏。

下载中提示

有下载在进行时，Downloads 标签显示小红点。进入下载页即可看到所有排队与进行中的任务 （IN PROGRESS 区域，含进度与取消按钮），已完成文件在下方。

已下载内容

库顶部有 Downloaded 区域，点一下即可离线播放；Manage 进入下载页。 下载页按最近下载时间排序。

刷新

库页面下拉或点刷新按钮。启动时、离开 15 分钟后回到前台也会自动刷新，使用条件请求，省流量。

播放

  • 后台播放：锁屏或切 App 继续。
  • 锁屏 / 控制中心：播放暂停、后退 15 秒、前进 15 秒、上一集/下一集。
  • 耳机：拔出暂停；来电结束后恢复。
  • 倍速：播放页胶囊或设置里调整（0.8×–2×）。
  • 续播：每 5 秒保存一次；不足 10 秒视为从头。
  • Up Next：可加入队列，播放器列出后续单集，点一下即跳播。
  • 剩余时间：进度条右侧显示 -mm:ss。

下载

  • 点单集右侧箭头，进度显示在行内与 Downloads 页。
  • 断网或重启 App 后可续传。
  • 播放优先使用本地文件，离线也能听。
  • 同一行可取消；已完成的用垃圾桶删除。
  • Settings → Delete All Downloads 清空音频；Clear Artwork Cache 清封面缓存。

收藏与标签栏

  • 底部左侧胶囊是 Library、Favorites、Downloads；右下角浮动按钮打开设置。
  • 主屏左右滑动可在 Library → Favorites → Downloads 之间循环切换；切换时胶囊里的玻璃高光会滑过去，页面有一道柔和光带掠过。
  • 播放时点右上角的心形，把这一集加入收藏。
  • 进入 Favorites 点条目即可播放，其余收藏会排进队列。
  • 向下滚动时胶囊先收缩成左侧一个图标（与右侧设置左右对称），继续下滑两个图标一起隐藏，列表占满底部；上滑先浮现图标，点图标或回顶恢复完整胶囊。

Erste Schritte

Podcast abonnieren

  1. Mediathek öffnen und + tippen.
  2. RSS-Feed-URL einfügen (z. B. https://feeds.npr.org/510289/podcast.xml).
  3. Abonnieren tippen; PodNook lädt den Feed einmal und speichert ihn lokal.

Eine Website-Adresse funktioniert nicht – es muss ein RSS-/Atom-Feed sein.

OPML importieren

  1. Abos in der anderen App als OPML exportieren.
  2. Datei in „Dateien“ ablegen oder in Dateien → Auf meinem iPhone → PodNook.
  3. PodNook → Einstellungen → OPML importieren, Datei wählen.
  4. Import mit 3 parallelen Abrufen; vorhandene Abos werden übersprungen, defekte Feeds am Ende gelistet.

Sendung umbenennen

Sendung öffnen und den Stift im Kopfbereich tippen. „Originalnamen verwenden“ stellt den Feed-Titel wieder her; der eigene Name gilt in der Mediathek, auf der Sendungsseite und auf dem Sperrbildschirm.

Laufende Downloads

Läuft ein Download, zeigt die Downloads-Schaltfläche einen roten Punkt. Dort stehen alle wartenden und laufenden Übertragungen unter „In Arbeit“ mit Fortschritt und Abbrechen; fertige Dateien darunter.

Geladene Folgen

Heruntergeladene Folgen erscheinen oben in der Mediathek unter Geladen – ein Tipp startet die Offline-Wiedergabe. Verwalten öffnet die Download-Ansicht, die nach zuletzt geladen sortiert ist.

Aktualisieren

In der Mediathek nach unten ziehen oder den Aktualisieren-Button tippen. Automatisch beim Start und nach 15 Minuten, mit bedingten Anfragen (kaum Datenverbrauch bei unveränderten Sendungen).

Wiedergabe

  • Hintergrund: läuft bei gesperrtem Bildschirm oder in anderen Apps weiter.
  • Sperrbildschirm & Kontrollzentrum: Play/Pause, 15 s zurück/vor, vorherige/nächste Folge.
  • Kopfhörer: Abziehen pausiert; nach einem Anruf geht es weiter.
  • Tempo: Chip im Player oder Einstellungen (0,8×–2×).
  • Fortsetzen: alle 5 s gesichert; unter 10 s startet von vorn.
  • Als Nächstes: Folgen zur Warteschlange hinzufügen; Tippen springt direkt.
  • Restzeit: rechts neben dem Regler als -mm:ss.

Downloads

  • Pfeil an der Folge tippen; Fortschritt erscheint in der Zeile und in Downloads.
  • Setzt nach Netzverlust oder App-Neustart fort.
  • Wiedergabe nutzt die lokale Datei – offline ohne Netz.
  • Laufende Downloads in der Zeile abbrechen, fertige mit dem Papierkorb löschen.
  • Einstellungen → Alle Downloads löschen; Cover-Cache leeren.

Favoriten & Tableiste

  • Die Pille unten links enthält Mediathek, Favoriten und Downloads; der schwebende Knopf rechts unten öffnet die Einstellungen.
  • Im Hauptbildschirm nach links/rechts wischen: Mediathek → Favoriten → Downloads → Mediathek; dabei gleitet ein Glas-Highlight zum neuen Tab und ein weicher Schimmer läuft über die Seite.
  • Herz im Player antippen, um die Folge zu den Favoriten zu legen.
  • Im Favoriten-Tab startet ein Tipp die Folge, der Rest der Liste wird eingereiht.
  • Beim Scrollen nach unten schrumpft die Leiste zu einem Symbol links (spiegelbildlich zu den Einstellungen rechts), dann verschwinden beide Symbole und die Liste nutzt den unteren Rand. Hochscrollen holt die Symbole zurück, Tippen oder ganz nach oben die volle Leiste.

Actualiser

Lecture

Téléchargements

Download in corso

Durante un download la scheda Download mostra un punto rosso. Aprila per vedere tutti i trasferimenti in attesa e in corso sotto “In corso”, con progresso e annullamento.

Episodi scaricati

Gli episodi scaricati compaiono in cima alla Libreria nella sezione Scaricati — un tocco per ascoltare offline. Gestisci apre Download, ordinati per download più recente.

Aggiornare

Trascina la libreria verso il basso o tocca il pulsante di aggiornamento. Aggiorna anche all’avvio e dopo 15 minuti, con richieste condizionali (pochi dati se nulla è cambiato).

Riproduzione

  • Background: continua a schermo bloccato o in altre app.
  • Schermo bloccato e Centro di Controllo: play/pausa, −15 s, +15 s, precedente/successivo.
  • Cuffie: scollegandole va in pausa; dopo una chiamata riprende.
  • Velocità: pastiglia nel player o Impostazioni (0,8×–2×).
  • Ripresa: progresso salvato ogni 5 s; sotto 10 s riparte da zero.
  • In coda: aggiungi episodi; il player mostra i successivi e salta al tocco.
  • Tempo rimanente: a destra sotto il cursore come -mm:ss.

Download

  • Tocca la freccia sull’episodio; il progresso appare nella riga e in Download.
  • Riprende dopo perdita di rete o riavvio dell’app.
  • La riproduzione usa il file locale: offline senza rete.
  • Annulla i download in corso dalla riga; elimina i completati col cestino.
  • Impostazioni → Elimina tutti i download; Svuota cache copertine.

Preferiti e schede

  • La pastiglia in basso a sinistra contiene Libreria, Preferiti e Download; il pulsante flottante in basso a destra apre le impostazioni.
  • Scorri a sinistra o a destra: Libreria → Preferiti → Download → Libreria; un riflesso di vetro scorre verso la nuova scheda e una luce attraversa la pagina.
  • Tocca il cuore nel player per tenere un episodio nei preferiti.
  • Nella scheda Preferiti un tocco avvia l’episodio e accoda il resto dell’elenco.
  • Scorrendo verso il basso la pastiglia si riduce a un’icona a sinistra (simmetrica alle impostazioni a destra), poi entrambe spariscono e l’elenco usa il fondo schermo. Scorrendo verso l’alto tornano; tocca l’icona o vai in cima per la pastiglia completa.

Risoluzione problemi

SintomoCosa provare
“Aggiornamento non riuscito”Il server non risponde; aggiorna più tardi, il badge sparisce dopo un aggiornamento riuscito.
Download “Link expired”URL con token temporaneo; aggiorna il feed e riprova.
Download si interrompeRiprova; riprende dai byte già scaricati o ricomincia se l’host non supporta la ripresa.
L’episodio non parteControlla la connessione; alcuni host bloccano client non browser o servono solo HTTP.
Import OPML vuotoUsa un file .opml/.xml con attributi xmlUrl.
Schermo nero “In iOS 14+, debug mode Flutter apps…”Build Debug avviata dalla home; avvia da Xcode/flutter run o installa una Release.
Nessun controllo a schermo bloccatoAvvia una volta la riproduzione nell’app per pubblicare “Now Playing”.
Copertine mancantiAlcuni CDN degli editori (per esempio NPR) rifiutano le richieste dirette con 403. Appare un segnaposto, riproduzione e download non sono coinvolti; appena l’immagine è raggiungibile viene mostrata e salvata per l’offline.
Copertine offlineLe copertine già viste e quelle degli episodi scaricati restano sul dispositivo e funzionano senza connessione.

FAQ

PodNook raccoglie dati? No — nessun account, tracciamento, pubblicità o analisi. Vedi Privacy.

Sincronizzazione? Non in 1.1, tutto è locale.

CarPlay, timer, capitoli? Non in 1.1; schermo bloccato e Centro di Controllo sono supportati.

Esportare le iscrizioni? L’import funziona, l’export è previsto.

Spazio occupato? Lo mostra Impostazioni; un’ora è di solito 30–60 MB.

Eliminando l’app? Libreria, progressi e download vengono rimossi.

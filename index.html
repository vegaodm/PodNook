Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no tracking, no ads, no analytics SDK.
  • Your subscriptions, listening progress and downloads stay on this device.
  • Network requests happen only for the feeds and audio you choose.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything. There is no server-side copy.

1. Who we are

PodNook is an independent podcast player for iOS, published under the bundle identifier com.vega.podnook.app. In this policy, “PodNook”, “we” and “us” refer to the developer of the app.

Questions about privacy can be sent to vegaodm@outlook.com.

None. PodNook does not create an account, does not ask for your name, email or phone number, and does not include any analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting SDK.

3. Data stored on your device

PodNook keeps the following inside its own iOS sandbox:

  • Feeds you subscribed to: feed URL, title, author, artwork URL, description, HTTP cache headers.
  • Episodes: titles, show notes, audio URLs, durations, publication dates, listening position, finished flag.
  • Your queue, playback speed and library settings (for example how many episodes to keep per show).
  • Downloaded audio files and cached artwork.

These files live in the app’s private “Application Support” and “Caches” containers. Deleting the app removes them. iOS backups may include them if you have device backups enabled; that backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

The only folder exposed to the Files app is a small import folder named PodNook, used to read OPML subscription exports that you place there yourself.

4. What leaves your device

PodNook talks to the internet only to do what you asked:

  • Refreshing a feed — requests the RSS/Atom URL you added. The podcast host (or its CDN) sees your IP address, a PodNook/1.0 (iOS) user agent, the request time, and conditional headers such as If-None-Match.
  • Downloading an episode or artwork — requests the media URL from the feed, with HTTP Range headers when resuming a download.
  • Streaming an episode — the audio player connects directly to the media URL.
  • Opening a link — the Privacy Policy and Support links open in Safari, which is subject to the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and to your browser settings.

We do not operate a server, do not proxy your requests, and never send your library, listening history or device information to us or to any third party.

5. Background audio

PodNook declares the iOS audio background mode so playback continues when you lock the screen or switch apps. That mode only keeps the audio session alive. PodNook does not request access to the microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos, health data or motion data.

6. Files and OPML import

When you import an OPML file, PodNook reads the file you selected (or a file you placed in its Files folder) on-device, extracts feed URLs, and stores them locally. The file and its contents are never uploaded anywhere.

7. Third-party components

PodNook is built with open-source software, including Flutter, just_audio, audio_service, audio_session, sqflite, Dio, the Dart xml package, file_picker and url_launcher. These libraries run inside the app and do not collect data for us. Each remains under its own license.

Podcast audio, artwork and show notes belong to the respective publishers; their own privacy practices apply when your device contacts their servers.

8. Retention and deleting your data

  • Unsubscribe from a show: its episodes, queue entries and downloads are removed.
  • Settings → Delete All Downloads removes downloaded audio files.
  • Settings → Clear Artwork Cache removes cached images.
  • Delete the app: the entire library, database and downloads are removed by iOS.

Because there is no account and no server copy, nothing else needs to be deleted.

9. Children

PodNook is not directed to children under 13. It plays third-party podcasts whose content we do not moderate or control; shows may contain explicit language or mature themes. The App Store age rating reflects this.

10. Security

All data stays in the iOS app sandbox. Network requests use HTTPS whenever the feed or media host supports it. PodNook does not store passwords, tokens or payment details, because it never asks for them.

11. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that changes how data is handled (for example optional sync), this page will be updated first and the date at the top will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

3. 保存在你设备上的数据

以下内容存放在 App 自己的 iOS 沙盒内：

  • 你订阅的节目：feed 地址、标题、作者、封面地址、简介、HTTP 缓存头。
  • 单集信息：标题、节目简介、音频地址、时长、发布时间、收听进度、是否听完。
  • 播放队列、倍速、库设置（例如每个节目保留多少集）。
  • 已下载的音频文件和封面缓存。

这些文件位于 App 私有的 “Application Support” 与 “Caches” 目录。删除 App 即一并删除。 若你开启了 iCloud/本机备份，备份中可能包含它们；备份由你和 Apple 控制，与我们无关。

唯一对“文件”App 可见的目录是一个名为 PodNook 的导入文件夹，用于读取你主动放入的 OPML 订阅文件。

4. 哪些数据会离开设备

PodNook 只在完成你要求的操作时联网：

  • 刷新订阅：请求你添加的 RSS/Atom 地址。播客托管方（或其 CDN）会看到你的 IP、 用户代理 PodNook/1.0 (iOS)、请求时间，以及 If-None-Match 之类的条件请求头。
  • 下载单集或封面：请求 feed 中的媒体地址，续传时附带 HTTP Range 头。
  • 在线播放：播放器直接连接媒体地址。
  • 打开链接：隐私政策与支持页面会在 Safari 中打开，受托管方（GitHub Pages）与浏览器设置约束。

我们没有服务器，不代理你的请求，也不会把你的订阅、收听记录或设备信息发送给我们或任何第三方。

5. 后台播放

PodNook 声明了 iOS 的音频后台模式，用于锁屏或切换 App 时继续播放。该模式仅维持音频会话， 不申请麦克风、相机、定位、通讯录、照片、健康或运动数据的权限。

6. 文件与 OPML 导入

导入 OPML 时，PodNook 在你选择的文件（或你放入其“文件”文件夹的文件）中本机读取并提取订阅地址， 随后保存在本地。文件内容不会上传到任何地方。

7. 第三方组件

PodNook 使用开源软件构建，包括 Flutter、just_audio、audio_service、audio_session、sqflite、Dio、 Dart xml 库、file_picker 与 url_launcher。这些库在 App 内运行，不为我们收集数据， 各自遵循其开源许可证。

播客音频、封面与节目简介的版权归各发布方所有；当设备访问其服务器时，适用对方自己的隐私做法。

8. 数据保留与删除

  • 取消订阅：该节目的单集、队列项与下载文件一并删除。
  • 设置 → Delete All Downloads：删除全部已下载音频。
  • 设置 → Clear Artwork Cache：删除封面缓存。
  • 删除 App：iOS 会移除整个订阅库、数据库与下载文件。

因为没有账号也没有服务器副本，不存在需要额外清除的数据。

10. 安全

所有数据保存在 iOS App 沙盒内；当托管方支持时，网络请求使用 HTTPS。 PodNook 不保存密码、令牌或支付信息，因为从不索取这些信息。

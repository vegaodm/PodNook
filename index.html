Privacy Policy

Version 1.1 · Last updated: November 23, 2026

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no tracking, no ads, no analytics SDK.
  • Your subscriptions, listening progress and downloads stay on this device.
  • Network requests happen only for the feeds and audio you choose.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything. There is no server-side copy.

1. Who we are

PodNook is an independent podcast player for iOS, published under the bundle identifier com.vega.podnook.app. “PodNook”, “we” and “us” refer to the developer of the app. Questions: vegaodm@outlook.com.

2. Data we collect

None. PodNook has no account system, does not ask for your name, email or phone number, and includes no analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting SDK.

3. Data stored on your device

  • Feeds you subscribed to: URL, title, author, artwork URL, description, HTTP cache headers.
  • Episodes: titles, show notes, audio URLs, durations, dates, listening position, finished flag.
  • Queue, playback speed and library settings (for example how many episodes to keep per show).
  • Downloaded audio files and cached artwork.

These live in the app’s private containers. Deleting the app removes them. Device backups are controlled by you and Apple. The only folder exposed to the Files app is a small import folder named PodNook for OPML files you place there.

4. What leaves your device

PodNook uses the network only to do what you asked:

  • Refreshing a feed — the podcast host sees your IP, a PodNook/1.1 (iOS) user agent, the request time and conditional headers such as If-None-Match.
  • Downloading or streaming audio/artwork — requests go to the media URLs in the feed, with HTTP Range headers when resuming.
  • Opening a link — the Privacy and Support pages open in Safari, subject to GitHub Pages and your browser.

We run no server, do not proxy your requests, and never receive your library, history or device information.

5. Background audio, files and permissions

PodNook declares the iOS audio background mode so playback continues when the screen is locked. It does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos, health or motion access. OPML files you pick (or drop into the PodNook folder) are read on-device only.

6. Third-party components

Built with open-source software: Flutter, just_audio, audio_service, audio_session, sqflite, Dio, the Dart xml package, file_picker and url_launcher. They run inside the app and collect nothing for us. Podcast audio, artwork and show notes belong to their publishers; their own practices apply when your device contacts their servers.

7. Retention and deleting your data

  • Unsubscribe: the show’s episodes, queue entries and downloads are removed.
  • Settings → Delete All Downloads / Clear Artwork Cache remove files and images.
  • Delete the app: iOS removes the whole library, database and downloads.

No account and no server copy means nothing else needs deleting.

8. Children, security and changes

PodNook is not directed to children under 13. It plays third-party podcasts we neither moderate nor control; shows may contain explicit language or mature themes, which is reflected in the age rating.

All data stays in the iOS sandbox; network requests use HTTPS where the host supports it. PodNook stores no passwords, tokens or payment details. If a future version changes data handling, this page is updated first and the date above changes.
